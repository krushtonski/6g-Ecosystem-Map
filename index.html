    Key people

    How are key people identified? People are ranked by verified leadership roles, funded awards, standards governance and cross-sector collaboration, all taken from the public sources behind each link.
    +3 named PI or lead on a funding or award link · +3 chair, vice-chair or board role · +2 links to organisations in 2 or more sectors · +2 joint R&D or testbed project · +1 event speaker · +2 leadership title matching one of four types: Funding Gatekeeper (programme directors and grant leads at funders), Standards Leader (chairs and rapporteurs in 3GPP, ITU-R, O-RAN, Next G Alliance), Testbed Lead (PIs, lab and testbed directors), Ecosystem Connector (technology and 6G strategy leads, board members, regional connectors).
